Third & mixed conditionals

8 min

Gillian Lynne's story is perfect for talking about how the past could have been different:

Third: unreal past → past result

If + had V3, would have V3

If the doctor hadn't noticed her talent, she might have been put on medication.

Mixed: past → present

If + had V3, would + V

If I had studied art, I would be a designer now.

past cause, present result (now, today)

Mixed: present → past

If + V2, would have V3

If I were more patient, I wouldn't have quit the guitar.

a permanent trait caused a past result

Level up: inversion (formal, C1). Drop if and put had first:
If I had known → Had I known, I would have come.
If it hadn't been for the doctor → Had it not been for the doctor, she would never have danced.
Also: would → could / might for possibility: She might never have become a choreographer.

2. Sentence transformation (exam style)

Complete the second sentence so it means the same as the first.

  1. I didn't know you were in town, so I didn't call you.
    If I you were in town, I would have called you.
  2. He didn't take the job in London, so he still lives in Moscow.
    If he had taken the job in London, he in Moscow now.
  3. I'm not good at maths, so I failed the exam.
    If I good at maths, I wouldn't have failed the exam.
  4. We got lost because we didn't have a map.
    We lost if we had had a map.
  5. She ignored her teachers' advice, and now she's a famous artist.
    If she had listened to her teachers, she a famous artist now.
  6. If I had known, I would have come.
    known, I would have come.
  7. Thanks to the doctor, Gillian became a dancer.
    Had it not the doctor, Gillian wouldn't have become a dancer.

3. Sliding doors

Think of one decision or event that changed your life. Tell your partner what would be different now, and what would have happened if things had gone the other way. Use at least one of each type.

If I hadn't…, I would never have…If I had…, I'd probably be… now.Had it not been for…, I…

Debate

15 min
"Within 20 years, AI will make traditional schools unnecessary."

Zakhar argues FOR, Mikhail argues AGAINST. (Then swap sides for a second round!)

Format
  1. Preparation: 3 min. Write 3 arguments + 1 example for each.
  2. Opening statement: 2 min each.
  3. Rebuttal: 2 min each. Answer your opponent's points.
  4. Closing: 1 min each. Use one conditional sentence!
Making your point
I'd argue that…The key point here is…The evidence suggests…Take … for example.Let's not forget that…
Challenging
I see where you're coming from, but…That's a fair point, however…That may be true, but it doesn't mean…With all due respect…What you're missing is…

Other motions if you have time: "Schools should grade creativity." / "University is no longer worth the money." / "Homework should be banned."
