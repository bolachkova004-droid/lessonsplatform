Notice the grammar

Waldinger says: "For 75 years, we've tracked the lives of 724 men." and "So what have we learned?"

But when he describes the start of the study in 1938, he switches to the past: the men were teenagers, they grew up in different worlds, they went into all walks of life.

Why does he use two different tenses? Let's find out.

Present Perfect or Past Simple?

8 min

Step 1. Look at the time.

Past Simple: finished time

in 1938NOW
V2 / V-ed · did you…?
  • When? It's important or we know it: The study started in 1938.
  • Stories, details: They grew up in Boston.
  • yesterday, last year, in 2015, ago, when I was a child

Present Perfect: past + now

1938NOW
have / has + V3
  • From the past until now: We've tracked them for 75 years.
  • Experience in your life (no exact time): I've been to Italy.
  • Result now, news: I've lost my keys!
  • ever, never, already, yet, just, for, since, this week, today

Step 2. How real conversations work.

A: Have you ever been to Georgia? (experience: Present Perfect)
B: Yes, I have! (short answer)
A: Really? When did you go? (details: Past Simple)
B: I went there two years ago. The food was amazing!

Подсказка. В русском одно прошедшее время, поэтому спросите себя: важно, КОГДА это было (есть «вчера, в 2015, неделю назад»)? Тогда Past Simple. Важен результат или опыт к сегодняшнему дню, а точного времени нет? Тогда Present Perfect. Со словом ago никогда не бывает Present Perfect!

Step 3. Sort the time words.

  1. yesterday →
  2. ever →
  3. two days ago →
  4. already →
  5. in 2019 →
  6. since Monday →
  7. when I was a child →
  8. yet →

Let's talk

8 min

"Have you ever…?" chain

Ask a Have you ever…? question. If the answer is "yes", ask 2 follow-up questions in Past Simple: When…? Where…? Who with? What was it like?

Press the button to start
  • Have you ever lost touch with a good friend?
  • Have you ever made a friend online?
  • Have you ever lived in another city?
  • Have you ever helped a stranger?
  • Have you ever had a big argument with a friend and made up?
  • Have you ever done something really brave?
  • Have you ever met someone famous?
  • Have you ever changed your mind about something important?

Discussion

  1. Do you agree that relationships matter more than money or fame? Why?
  2. Who have you known the longest in your life? How did you meet?
  3. Waldinger suggests "replacing screen time with people time". How much screen time do you have? Is it a problem?
  4. What is one thing you could do this week to "lean in" to a relationship?

Homework

2 min
  1. Write 100–120 words: "A person who has made my life better". Use Present Perfect for the connection to now (I've known her for… / She has always…) and Past Simple for the story (We met in… / She helped me when…).
  2. Watch the talk again with English subtitles. Write down 5 phrases you like.
  3. Grammar diary: every day write 2 sentences: one about something you have done today, and one about something you did yesterday.
