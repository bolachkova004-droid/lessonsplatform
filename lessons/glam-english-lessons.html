Как пользоваться

  • Check подсвечивает ответы зелёным или красным и показывает счёт. Enter в поле ввода тоже запускает проверку.
  • Show answers заполняет пропуски правильными ответами (синим цветом). Если отправляете урок как домашнее задание, предупредите ученика, что сначала нужно сделать самому.
  • В вопросах с вариантами ответа (True/False, видео) неверный вариант зачёркивается, а верный становится зелёным.
  • Карточки слов переворачиваются по клику. Кнопка Next question выдаёт случайный вопрос без повторов.
  • Таймеры (у Миланы и у Захара с Михаилом): кнопка Start запускает, повторное нажатие сбрасывает.
  • Если ученик закроет страницу, введённые ответы не сохранятся. Для домашки лучше просить прислать текст или скриншот.
  • Видео открывается на сайте TED в новой вкладке (встроить его в страницу нельзя). Если TED не грузится, найдите то же видео на YouTube по названию.

Только начала заниматься, неуверенная. Урок построен так, чтобы у неё всё получалось: сначала ваш пример, потом её ответ, много опорных фраз. Заодно проводим needs analysis.

Confessions of a Night Owl

a blog post by Lily, 29

Hi, I'm Lily, and I'm a night owl. My friends are usually in bed by eleven, but I rarely go to sleep before two. I'm not lazy. My brain just works better at night!

On weekdays I work from home, so I don't need to get up early. I usually wake up at around ten, grab a coffee and check my emails. In the afternoon I run errands or meet friends. My most productive time starts at about 9 p.m. That's when I write, plan and get creative.

At the weekend I take it easy. I sleep in, I catch up with friends and sometimes I binge-watch a good series.

This month, though, things are different. I'm learning Spanish because I'm going to Madrid in the summer, so I'm trying to go to bed earlier and study in the mornings. It's really hard! Right now, as I'm writing this, it's 8 a.m. and I'm drinking my third coffee. Wish me luck!

1. Lily's friends go to bed later than she does.

2. Lily works from home.

3. She feels most creative in the evening.

4. At the weekend she gets up early.

5. This month she's changing her routine.

6. She's learning Spanish for her job.

Grammar detective

Present Simple or Present Continuous?

8 min

Present Simple

I work · she works · do you work?
  • habits and routines: I usually wake up at ten.
  • facts, things that are always true: My sister lives in Kazan.
  • signal words: always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day, on Mondays

Present Continuous

I'm working · she's working · are you working?
  • right now, at this moment: I'm drinking my third coffee.
  • temporary things, "these days": I'm learning Spanish this month.
  • signal words: now, right now, at the moment, today, this week, these days, Look!, Listen!
Careful! "State" verbs are usually not in Continuous: like, love, hate, want, need, know, understand, believe, remember, belong.
✗ I'm wanting a new phone.   ✓ I want a new phone.

Подсказка. Спросите себя: это обычно / всегда (Simple) или сейчас / в этот период (Continuous)? «Я работаю в банке» = I work. «Я сейчас работаю над проектом» = I'm working.

Notice the grammar

Waldinger says: "For 75 years, we've tracked the lives of 724 men." and "So what have we learned?"

But when he describes the start of the study in 1938, he switches to the past: the men were teenagers, they grew up in different worlds, they went into all walks of life.

Why does he use two different tenses? Let's find out.

Present Perfect or Past Simple?

8 min

Step 1. Look at the time.

Past Simple: finished time

in 1938NOW
V2 / V-ed · did you…?
  • When? It's important or we know it: The study started in 1938.
  • Stories, details: They grew up in Boston.
  • yesterday, last year, in 2015, ago, when I was a child

Present Perfect: past + now

1938NOW
have / has + V3
  • From the past until now: We've tracked them for 75 years.
  • Experience in your life (no exact time): I've been to Italy.
  • Result now, news: I've lost my keys!
  • ever, never, already, yet, just, for, since, this week, today

Step 2. How real conversations work.

A: Have you ever been to Georgia? (experience: Present Perfect)
B: Yes, I have! (short answer)
A: Really? When did you go? (details: Past Simple)
B: I went there two years ago. The food was amazing!

Подсказка. В русском одно прошедшее время, поэтому спросите себя: важно, КОГДА это было (есть «вчера, в 2015, неделю назад»)? Тогда Past Simple. Важен результат или опыт к сегодняшнему дню, а точного времени нет? Тогда Present Perfect. Со словом ago никогда не бывает Present Perfect!

Step 3. Sort the time words.

  1. yesterday →
  2. ever →
  3. two days ago →
  4. already →
  5. in 2019 →
  6. since Monday →
  7. when I was a child →
  8. yet →

Past Simple or Past Continuous?

8 min
we were waiting… (long)they announced (short)

Past Continuous: the background

was / were + V-ing
  • an action in progress: We were walking through tiny streets…
  • the "scene" of a story: It was raining.
  • often after while

Past Simple: the events

V2 / V-ed
  • short, finished actions: An old man stopped us.
  • one action after another: We arrived, went to the hotel and slept.
  • often after when
I / he / she / ityou / we / they
+I was sleepingWe were sleeping
−She wasn't sleepingThey weren't sleeping
?Was he sleeping?Were you sleeping?
Story formula: I was ___ing when something happened.  /  While I was ___ing, something happened.

Подсказка. Past Continuous отвечает на вопрос «что делал?» (процесс, фон). Past Simple отвечает на вопрос «что сделал?» (короткое действие, которое прервало процесс). «Я шла по улице, когда встретила друга» = I was walking down the street when I met a friend.

Third & mixed conditionals

Gillian Lynne's story is perfect for talking about how the past could have been different:

Third: unreal past → past result

If + had V3, would have V3

Mixed: past → present

If + had V3, would + V

If I had studied art, I would be a designer now.

past cause, present result (now, today)

Mixed: present → past

If + V2, would have V3

a permanent trait caused a past result
